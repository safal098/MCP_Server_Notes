Nepal is home to Mount Everest (Sagarmatha), the world's highest peak at 8,848.86 meters (29,031.7 feet).
Nepal's flag is the only non-quadrilateral national flag in the world, consisting of two triangular pennants.
The national animal of Nepal is the cow, which is considered sacred in Hinduism, the country's predominant religion.
Nepal transitioned from a monarchy to a federal democratic republic in 2008 after a decade-long civil war.
Nepal is culturally diverse with over 120 distinct ethnic groups and 123 different languages spoken across the country.
The Gurkhas, soldiers from Nepal, are renowned for their bravery and have served in the British Army since 1815.
Nepal experiences six distinct seasons: spring, summer, monsoon, autumn, pre-winter, and winter.
Momos, steamed dumplings filled with meat or vegetables, are a popular dish in Nepalese cuisine.
Nepal suffered a devastating earthquake in 2015 with a magnitude of 7.8, killing nearly 9,000 people and destroying numerous historic sites.
